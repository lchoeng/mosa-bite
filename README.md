# MOSA BITE!

An underwater monster-hunting arcade game for kids ages 3–7, starring **Linus** and **Wesley**.

**EAT. GROW. BITE. BECOME THE KING OF THE OCEAN!**

## Play
Open `index.html` in a browser (iPhone in landscape works best). With GitHub Pages turned on for this repo, play at `https://lchoeng.github.io/mosa-bite/` and use Safari's *Add to Home Screen* for full-screen.

## How to play
- **Touchpad (bottom-left):** swim in any direction, diagonals too.
- **BITE (bottom-right):** chomp creatures smaller than you. The button glows green when food is in reach. Tapping open water on the right also bites.
- **Special:** Linus = ⚡ Speed Burst, Wesley = 🦷 Super Bite (10-second recharge).
- Red rings mean a bigger predator: swim away!
- Grow through 5 stages: Baby Monster → Little Predator → Ocean Hunter → Super Predator → Ocean King.
- 5 oceans: Tropical Reef, Kelp Forest, Deep Ocean, Shark Territory, Monster Kingdom.
- Bosses: Mega Squid, Giant Hammerhead, Giant Shark, Sea Monster King. Dodge them, and bite when they get dizzy 💫.
- Power-ups: ⚡ Lightning Bubble, 😮 Giant Bite, 🫧 Protective Bubble, 🧲 Magnet Fish, ⭐ Growth Boost.
- Pause menu: music/sound volume and **Auto-Chomp** easy mode.

Keyboard: arrows/WASD to swim, Space to bite, E or Shift for the special, P to pause.

Single self-contained HTML file: all character and creature art is embedded.
